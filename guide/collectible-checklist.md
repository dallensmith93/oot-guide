## Gold Skulltulas

- [ ] **Gold Skulltula #4 of 100 - gs-004 — Compass alcove** (Deku Tree; Child; Any)
  - Where: Deku Tree - Compass alcove.
  - Requires: Slingshot. Raise the compass-room platforms with the switch; cross left and collect the south-wall spider.
