# The 25-episode playthrough

Episodes preserve the existing chronological route and all stable objective IDs. Length varies with dungeon and minigame retries.

## Episode 01: A hero in the forest

### [Kokiri Forest: sword, shield and supplies](walkthrough.md#r01)

1. Start at Link’s house. Go southwest to the training clearing and stone arch. Follow the dark passage, crawl beneath the slabs, climb the rope ladder and jump to the chest ledge for the Kokiri Sword. Return by the stone steps.

2. Gather rupees from Mido’s house chests, grass and the village’s ledges. Buy a Deku Shield for 40 rupees at the northeast shop; equip both sword and shield, then show Mido to pass east to the Deku Tree.

3. Cut the Deku Babas on the way for sticks and nuts. A straight stalk yields a stick; a defeated writhing plant yields nuts. Keep sticks for lighting webs in the dungeon.

4. Open the village chests as you pass; do not spend time farming a full wallet. Buy nothing else before the first dungeon.

### [Great Deku Tree: clear the whole first dungeon](walkthrough.md#r02)

1. Climb the central ramp: open the Map chest beside the vine wall. Shield-reflect the Scrub’s seed in the upper door. Cross the collapsing platform and climb to the Slingshot chest; open the small side-vine chest. Shoot the switch above the exit to lower the escape platform.

2. Shoot the spiders off the main vines; climb to 3F. In the west switch room raise the platforms, collect the alcove token, Compass and the small chest through the crawlspace. Relight the exit torch with a stick.

3. Jump from 3F into the centre of the floor web. Collect both basement tokens. Press the torch switch and open the web-covered chest. Carry fire to the south doorway; reflect the Scrub and shoot the eye above the exit.

4. Dive onto the underwater switch to lower the water beneath the rotating log; ride the platform through. Move the next block to the high door. Light both torches, defeat the falling larvae, and burn both webs: the side passage now leads straight to the fourth token, without a Boomerang revisit.

5. Crawl back to the upper basement ledge, push the block down, fetch torch fire and burn the floor web. Reflect the three Scrubs middle, right, left. Enter Gohma’s door.

## Episode 02: The princess and the forest song

### [Castle Town and Zelda: the new bottle errand](walkthrough.md#r03)

1. Leave Kokiri Forest west; Saria gives you the Fairy Ocarina. Cross Hyrule Field north to Castle Town. Cross the drawbridge and take the first door on the right. Smash the guardhouse pots for about 20 to 30 rupees when the room is full. Roll into the moving crate for its token.

2. In the east Potion Shop accept the borrowed bottle, catch the shop’s crawling bug, and show it to the merchant. You keep the bottle, with Red Potion: this is the remake’s additional bottle.

3. Talk to Malon in the market and then by the castle approach to receive the Weird Egg. Climb the vines behind her and bypass the gate along the upper path. Stay outside the guards’ view; cross the moat and use the hatched Cucco to wake Talon at the crates.

4. Move the milk crates to reach the crawlspace. Cross the garden using hedges and moving guards as cover. Speak to Zelda, accept her Letter, then follow Impa to learn Zelda’s Lullaby.

5. Before departing at night, bring the white dog near the market stalls to its owner in the back alley for the associated Heart Piece. Do the daytime shooting game in r09 with the ranch visit.

### [Lost Woods: Saria, target game and stick capacity](walkthrough.md#r04)

1. If the Potion Shop bottle still contains Red Potion, drink it when useful, then catch a bug beside the broken field cart or under a loose rock. Keep a bug ready before releasing it into any soft soil; collect another before the next patch.

2. From Kokiri Forest climb the northwest ledge and vines into the Lost Woods. At the first right, hit the hanging target’s centre three times; every shot must score 100. This grants a seed-capacity upgrade. Drop down and copy the Skull Kids’ increasingly long music phrases for their Heart Piece.

3. From the forest entrance, follow right, left, right, left, straight, left, right to the Sacred Grove. Defeat the Wolfos, work through the hedge maze, and meet Saria at the far steps for Saria’s Song.

4. Return to the Lost Woods entrance and take the first left. Play Saria’s Song for the lone Skull Kid’s Heart Piece. Continue left to the bridge overlook and defeat the Business Scrub by reflecting its seed; buy the stick-capacity upgrade for 40 rupees.

5. Keep a bottled bug for the soil patches listed with this visit. The Forest Stage mask rewards wait until r12, when the mask circuit and Boomerang pickups can be combined.

## Episode 03: Village secrets and the Gorons

### [Kakariko and Graveyard: Cuccos, Sun’s Song and digging](walkthrough.md#r05)

1. Return all seven Kakariko Cuccos: central construction site; Hyrule Field entrance; below Death Mountain steps; the brick pile west of House of Skulltula; wobbling crate by the northern ground-level house; behind the eastern fence; and the high ledge reached by that fenced area’s ladder. Carry a second Cucco from high ground to glide onto the brick pile and over the windmill-side fence; toss the trapped birds out before leaving.

2. Use a Cucco to reach the high blue-roof house and speak to the man for the rooftop Heart Piece before returning the last bird. The Cucco lady awards the third bottle in this route.

3. Show Zelda’s Letter to the north gate guard to open Death Mountain and start the Happy Mask Shop lead.

4. At the back of the Graveyard play Zelda’s Lullaby on the Royal Tomb’s Triforce. Defeat the Keese, cross past the ReDeads and read the rear inscription for Sun’s Song.

5. At night pull the marked southeastern graves: take the Hylian Shield from its chest; in the far back-right grave take the relocated Heart Piece. During Dampe’s early-night digging shift, pay at soft-soil spots until he uncovers the Heart Piece; accept extra digging when he finds something hard. Pick up the reward immediately.

6. Use Sun’s Song for the village night tokens. Claim the 10-token wallet reward as soon as your checked token count qualifies. Talk to the family to register the House of Skulltula quest, the additional sidequest omitted from some six-quest lists.

1. Bomb the river entrance rocks. Buy Magic Beans from the seated seller; prices rise with each purchase, so buy the beans needed for the current soil stops and the coming cleanup instead of emptying the wallet indiscriminately. Use a Cucco to reach the river’s elevated Heart Pieces.

2. At the waterfall stand on the Triforce platform and play Zelda’s Lullaby. In the Domain climb to King Zora and pay the diving-game Zora. Jump from the waterfall and gather all marked rupees underwater for the Silver Scale.

3. Dive into the underwater tunnel opposite the Domain waterfall to Lake Hylia. With the scale, collect the submerged bottle containing Ruto’s Letter. Plant a bean outside the Lakeside Laboratory and visit the scarecrows: compose a memorable eight-note tune with Bonooru and record it exactly for the adult return.

4. Return through the tunnel, show the letter to King Zora, and keep the emptied bottle. Light the Domain’s torch chain, ending behind the waterfall, for its Heart Piece; put away the stick between torches when necessary.

5. Catch a small fish in a bottle near the Domain shallows. Pass the king into Zora Fountain; offer the fish in front of Jabu-Jabu to enter. The nearby fountain and outdoor tokens are combined with the post-dungeon visit.

## Episode 06: Inside Jabu-Jabu

### [Lord Jabu-Jabu’s Belly: Ruto and Barinade](walkthrough.md#r11)

1. Shoot the ceiling tonsil; follow Ruto through the floor hole. Speak until she permits carrying. Carry her south, throw her onto the far ledge of the water recess, press its switch, then collect the Map in the side alcove and vine token. Continue south and ride the moving lift back up.

2. Carry Ruto through the hole room to the five-way corridor. Take the left switch branch, defeat the flying Stingers with the Slingshot and obtain the Boomerang. Back outside, leave Ruto on the east weight switch and defeat the red tentacle. Clear the northwest blue tentacle, northeast bubble room for Compass, then central green tentacle.

3. Drop through the newly clear northwest floor hole; collect both ledge/passage tokens. Throw Ruto onto the stone platform. Stun Bigocto, circle behind and stun its rear again before sword attacks; repeat until the lift rises.

4. Boomerang the wobbling platforms, carry a block to the weight switch, then climb the last vines for the final token and throw the Boomerang around the obstacle into the ceiling switch. Proceed to Barinade.

## Episode 07: Masks and the Door of Time

### [Child circuit: masks, upgrades and Boomerang pickups](walkthrough.md#r12)

1. At Zora Fountain bomb the rear rock wall; play Zelda’s Lullaby and pay 100 rupees for Farore’s Wind. Collect the high night token with the Boomerang before leaving.

2. In the southeastern Hyrule Field marsh, bomb open the cracked-rock grotto and defeat the ordinary Skulltula inside while a child. This sets up an adult crate containing its Gold Skulltula; return in r24. Without the child kill, the adult crate will not appear.

3. Sweep the Boomerang-only night pickups at Lake Hylia, Lon Lon Ranch and behind Dampe’s hut. Plant beans at the laboratory, Graveyard, Dodongo entrance, Kokiri shop soil and both Lost Woods soils after flushing their tokens with bugs.

4. From the Lost Woods entrance go right, left, right, left, left; enter the butterfly-marked Forest Stage hole. Wear Skull Mask for the stick upgrade. Return to the entrance’s first-left Skull Kid, sell the mask, repay the shop and borrow Spooky Mask. Sell that to the Graveyard child by day, repay and borrow Bunny Hood.

5. After all three Stones the Running Man is in Hyrule Field. Meet him when he stops to rest and sell Bunny Hood for the remake’s 200-rupee reward. Repay the mask shop to unlock Mask of Truth and the three cosmetic race masks. Borrow Mask of Truth, show it at the Forest Stage and speak to the leader for the nut-capacity reward.

6. Bomb the boulder just outside the Sacred Grove for the other nut upgrade. In Goron City stop the large rolling Goron with a bomb in his tunnel for the other Bomb Bag upgrade. In Castle grounds visit the bombable fountain and pay 50 rupees for the magical spin enhancement, and complete the child fishing reward described in the attached collectible.

## Episode 08: Seven years later

### [Adult essentials: Epona, Hookshot and Storms](walkthrough.md#r14)

1. Before the next childhood visit, read both remake age-linked Fairy Thoughts in Hyrule Field: the tree to the east/right of Castle Town drawbridge by the nearby grotto teaches its Scrub password; inspect the chest and rock-overhang inscription near Gerudo Valley. These readings permit the child rewards collected during r17; merely knowing an online password is not the required in-game flag.

2. At Lon Lon Ranch pay Ingo’s riding fee, play Epona’s Song and mount Epona. Speak to Ingo while riding to start his wager. Win twice by taking the inside line and using carrots one at a time, keeping one available for overtaking; when locked in, jump the fence with a straight run-up. Epona is now yours.

3. In Kakariko accept the Pocket Egg, wait for dawn or use Sun’s Song, then use the Pocket Cucco on Talon in the central house. Return the happy bird for Cojiro; keep Cojiro until the planned full trade chain in r23.

4. In the Graveyard pull the tomb marked with a shovel/flowers and follow Dampe’s ghost through his race. Avoid his flames, cut tight corners and open the Hookshot chest. Play Song of Time at the blue blocks on the way out to emerge in the Windmill.

5. Take the Windmill Heart Piece from the rotating platform and show Guru-Guru the ocarina to learn Song of Storms. Re-enter Dampe’s race for the timed Heart Piece attempt while the layout is fresh; the final straight can be shortened with the Hookshot target.

6. Visit Lake Hylia to replay your exact child composition to Bonooru: Scarecrow Song becomes usable for adult Hookshot points. Return to the woods for Forest Temple.

### [Forest Temple: four Poes and Phantom Ganon](walkthrough.md#r15)

1. Play Saria’s Song for Mido in the Lost Woods. Pass the meadow Moblins from behind or stun with Hookshot; sidestep the large club strikes. Learn Minuet of Forest from Sheik and Hookshot to the entrance.

## Episode 10: Back to childhood

### [Return to childhood: Well and Hall access](walkthrough.md#r16)

1. Return to the Temple of Time; learn Prelude of Light from Sheik, then replace the sword to become a child. Approach Kakariko from Hyrule Field to hear Kaepora Gaebora explain the Hall of Fallen Foes. A glowing-green bed, including the Grand Lodge bed, now lets you sleep and dream there.

2. In the Windmill play Song of Storms to drain the well. Drop in, defeat the spider and walk through the false wall. Circle to the north fountain, play Zelda’s Lullaby to drain the inner pools, then crawl through the drained passage and enter the Dead Hand trap.

3. Destroy a grasping hand or escape its grip, let Dead Hand lower its head, and strike. Take Lenses of Truth. Use them on the south wall to find the ladder and Purple Rupee chest; jump the revealed pit in the prior corridor for the Seed chest.

4. In the main loop take Compass behind the west inner false wall. Drop to the basement; bomb the torch-marked passage boulders for Map. Collect five Silver Crystals, including the one by that passage, to climb out.

5. Get the southeast false-wall key and burn the far coffin’s web in the western coffin room for another. Spend them on the two north-facing inner cells for their tokens. Take the northeast outer door, Lens past pits, open the Deku Shield chest, defeat the Like Like and take the third token.

6. Open the remaining B1 chests: east moat; southwest false wall; southwest inner cell guarded by a spider; the northwest corridor dead end behind a Lens-revealed wall and hidden pit (Blue Rupee). Defeat the large Green Bubble for its Hall statue before leaving.

## Episode 11: Fire Temple and crater rewards

### [Fire Temple: Goron rescues and the Hammer](walkthrough.md#r18)

1. In Kakariko win the adult Shooting Gallery’s ten targets with its fifteen arrows for the 40-arrow quiver. In Goron City bomb the rolling Goron near the Bomb Flowers, hear both subjects and receive the Goron Tunic. Pull Darunia’s statue, Hookshot the broken bridge, learn Bolero of Fire and descend to Fire Temple.

2. Take the western Darunia room’s platform switch and cell key. Back at entrance unlock east. In the rope-bridge hall free the northern cell for a key; take the raised platform and move the Time Block for the tile/Like Like token room. Bomb the south wall for another cell key.

3. Unlock the east route, then its inner door; push the block onto the flame column and ride it up. Move the upper block beneath the climbable wall, hit the distant switch and climb while flames are off.

4. In the boulder maze take the south Map chest, northeast bombable-wall token and northwest cell key. Unlock the west exit; shoot the eye for Compass. Follow the chasing-flame platforms to the upper maze. Bomb the cracked floor for a cell key; the upper southwest switch frees another key cell.

5. Before leaving use Scarecrow Song at the upper maze entrance, Hookshot to Pierre and the lift, collect the chain-wall and upper lava tokens. Hit the 5F switch and Hookshot the posts quickly for all three timed rupee chests.

6. Return through the flame room’s locked west door. In the fire maze collect the north cell key, unlock the inner south door, carry a block onto the flame-lowering switch and use the next key. Bomb Flare Dancer to expose its small body; intercept it with sword attacks and ride the lift.

7. Drop a bomb or shoot the next flame switch, climb, then race the narrow inside ramp for the Megaton Hammer. Hammer the giant floor block, clear side totems, hammer the stairs and continue dropping the face blocks. Move the Time Block, hammer the cell switch for the last Small Key, then hammer the large pillar down to the boss-door room.

8. Return to the first room and hammer away the ground-level east blocks. Spend the remaining key; clear slugs, tiles, Like Like and the token behind hammer blocks. Defeat the second Flare Dancer, open its rupee chest and hammer the last Goron switch for Boss Key. Cross the lowered pillar to Volvagia.

## Episode 12: The frozen domain

### [Ice Cavern: Blue Fire and Iron Boots](walkthrough.md#r20)

1. Enter the frozen Domain and pass King Zora into Zora Fountain. Cross the ice floes for the high Heart Piece and follow the northern floes to the cavern.

2. Defeat all Freezards, then collect the five Silver Crystals around the spinning blade: three on the floor, one behind the near-left stalagmites, one from the high exit ledge. In the raised-walkway room break the ice around the Blue Fire and bottle it; melt the Map chest’s red ice.

3. Back at the blade room thaw the eastern passage for Compass, a token and another Blue Fire font. Melt the side red ice for jars and its Fairy Thought. Refill the bottles before taking the western passage.

4. In the block room clear Ice Keese first. Relative to entering, slide forward and take the first high rupee, then reset by pushing off the edge. From reset slide left, forward, right, forward, left, back, right, taking the high rupees as reached; thaw the final ledge rupee. For the optional Red Rupee chest, divert forward after the second left, refill at the fire font and play Song of Time beneath the high ledge.

5. To reach the exit, repeat through the first right, then push back against the tall block and left beneath the exit ledge. Melt the corridor ice, refill Blue Fire, defeat White Wolfos and take Iron Boots. Learn Serenade of Water.

6. Use the boots’ submerged exit to Zora Fountain. Thaw King Zora with Blue Fire for Zora Tunic. Keep another Blue Fire bottle for the frozen shop or any missed ice barriers, then warp to Lake Hylia.

## Episode 13: The Water Temple

### [Water Temple: one planned water-level cycle](walkthrough.md#r21)

1. Sink below Lake Hylia and Hookshot the entrance switch. In the hub sink to 1F and find Ruto’s passage, marked by the blue route. Float up for Map and play Zelda’s Lullaby to drain the temple. Light the two lower torches through the central flame; clear the Spikes for key 1.

2. Take the south cracked-wall water route and its rising Hookshot posts for Compass. Back in the hub push the west passage block into the water, sink through, stand on the whirlpool dragon, hit its mouth switch and pass the gate for key 2.

3. Spend one key on the central pillar. Hookshot up and play Lullaby at the green route to set water to 2F. Sink into the passage under the floating block, hit the switch and clear the falling enemies; float into the side ceiling opening for the Red Rupee chest and token. This is no longer an N64 Small Key chest.

4. At hub 2F bomb the east crack and pass the Stingers. Shoot the water-lid switch, stand on the lid and shoot again to rise for key 3. Return via Ruto’s submerged passage, float to its 2F cracked wall, bomb it and get the token.

5. Unlock west 2F at the pink route. Clear the Tektite, ride the switched water lid and play Lullaby for 3F. Use the last key on the west waterfall door, climb the moving Hookshot platforms and continue.

6. Dragon puzzle: raise heads and grapple southwest; lower and grapple the wall; circle and raise; grapple northwest, lower, climb onto the head and raise once more. Enter the misty tree arena; cross it, turn back and defeat Dark Link. Take Longshot.

7. Drop into the rapids, use Iron Boots to resist whirlpools and collect the corner token. Shoot the final gold eye, Longshot through the timed gate and return via the whirlpool room.

8. Reset to low using blue, then middle using green. Get the central pillar’s high token with Longshot now. At 2F north shoot the eye and Longshot through the gate. Use the ceiling target and underwater loop to approach the block twice: first pull, then circle back and push it aside to reach key 4.

9. At 1F north unlock the deep route. Cross the current room; bomb the cracked walls to pull its block onto the submerged switch. Cross the three water lids, wait for a boulder, then enter the waterfall corridor for the last token and Boss Key. Return to hub, follow pink to raise 3F, Longshot north and use Iron Boots on the spike-ramp approach.

## Episode 14: Lake rewards and Biggoron's Sword

### [Restored Lake Hylia: Fire Arrows and Golden Scale](walkthrough.md#r22)

1. At the warp-island plaque, face the morning sun and fire an ordinary arrow into its halo while the lake is full. Swim to the small platform and collect Fire Arrows. If dawn has passed, use Sun’s Song to cycle to the next sunrise.

2. Ride the laboratory bean to the roof Heart Piece. In the Fishing Hole pay the adult session fee of 30 rupees; catch a fish at least 60 cm, retain it and show the keeper for Golden Scale. Work the largest fish near the submerged log rather than repeatedly keeping small catches.

3. Fishing method: aim and cast beyond the fish, guide the lure into view with short stick movements, then reel once it bites. Steer with the resisting fish rather than hauling constantly in the opposite direction. Keep the record fish until the keeper accepts it.

4. At the Lakeside Laboratory dive unaided using Golden Scale to the bottom and speak to the scientist for the diving Heart Piece. Use Iron Boots separately to roll into the underwater crate for its token; boots do not count as the unaided diving feat.

5. At night collect the high tree token using Longshot/Scarecrow access. After Water Temple the Domain thaws, making its Lake Hylia underwater shortcut useful for the timed trade sequence next.

### [Biggoron Sword: finish all timed deliveries together](walkthrough.md#r23)

1. Bring Cojiro to the sleeping Master Craftsman’s Son in the Lost Woods’ first clearing. Show the bird for Odd Mushroom. Sources disagree between three and four minutes; plan a delivery under three minutes: leave the woods, call Epona, ride to Kakariko, enter the north potion shop and leave through its rear door to Granny’s shop. Trade the mushroom for Odd Poultice.

2. Return to the same woods clearing and give the poultice to Fado for Poacher’s Saw. Ride to Gerudo Valley, cross with Epona or Longshot and trade the saw to the carpenter boss by his tent for Broken Goron Sword.

3. Use Bolero of Fire before accepting any timed object. Leave the crater through the summit exit to Biggoron; trade the broken sword for Prescription. Bring the prescription to thawed King Zora for Eyeball Frog.

4. You have two and a half minutes for the frog. Immediately use the thawed Domain’s underwater tunnel to Lake Hylia, leave the water and enter the north laboratory. Give the frog to the scientist for Speciality Eye Drops.

5. You have three and a half minutes for the drops. Ride Epona to Kakariko; climb to Goron City, take Darunia’s opened statue passage into the crater and follow its summit exit to Biggoron. Deliver for the Receipt.

6. Wait three days, or cycle Sun’s Song six times, then show the Receipt to Biggoron for the unbreakable sword. This route uses warp songs only before timed deliveries; no unverified teleport timer shortcut is needed.

## Episode 16: Shadow Temple and regional cleanup

### [Shadow Temple: illusions, ferry and Bongo Bongo](walkthrough.md#r25)

1. Enter Kakariko for the burning-village scene and learn Nocturne of Shadow. Warp to the Graveyard ledge, reveal the invisible torch ring with Lenses of Truth and cast Din’s Fire at its centre.

2. Longshot over the opening gap. Follow the left false-wall route, keeping right through the first illusion maze, and defeat the enemies for Map. Continue west through the next false corridors; defeat Dead Hand for Hover Boots and return across the collapsed passage.

3. Point the bird statue at the Lens-visible true skull. Hover across its tongue gate. In the Beamos room take the southern false wall and clear Gibdos for Compass; north, collect all five blade-room Silver Crystals for a key. Bomb the east doorway and spend the key.

4. Cross the guillotines and defeat Stalfos. Take the invisible-platform east side room: clear enemies and open both rupee chests, including the invisible one, and collect its token. Return west via moving platforms; gather the cavern’s five Silver Crystals to open the spike-ceiling room.

5. Pull the Lens-hidden block beneath both falling spike panels. Climb across their tops for a key and token, opening the side chests too. Return north over the invisible moving platform and unlock. Lens the floor spikes, defeat the Gibdos and gather five Silver Crystals using hidden Hookshot targets.

6. In the skull room throw a bomb into the giant skull for a key; clear the Keese. Unlock the high exit. Wear Iron Boots against the fans, collect the pit token, then Longshot over. In the end ReDead room take the invisible Seed chest. Return and use the last fan with Hover Boots to cross into the invisible side passage; clear enemies for a key and open the opposite rupee chest.

7. Unlock to the ferry. Move the block beneath the ladder to create the shortcut; use Scarecrow Song from the deck for the high token. Play Zelda’s Lullaby on the ferry Triforce, defeat both Stalfos and jump off at its destination.

8. Burn the web and Lens the wall maze: south invisible Floormaster yields a key; west bomb the spinning skulls for token; north burn closing spike walls with Din’s Fire, defeat Gibdos and open Boss Key plus magic chests. Back at the dock bomb the left tower to bridge the crevice. Lens and Hover across the final platforms to the boss.

5. Leave Proving Grounds until r31, after Silver Gauntlets and Mirror Shield, so its paid admission can cover every room in one visit. The horseback rewards follow in r32 after the desert temples.

### [Ganondorf’s Castle: the other five barriers](walkthrough.md#r39)

1. Spirit trial: bomb Beamos and collect all five Silver Crystals, using the ceiling target for the airborne one and moving Armos to clear the spike paths. Clear the next slugs for the Seed chest.
